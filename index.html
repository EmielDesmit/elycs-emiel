Wasted Paris fusion capsule - collab 2024

I asked Google who was the photographer of the Wasted Paris Fusion capsule?

The photographer behind Wasted Paris's "Fusion" capsule collection is Belgian photographer Emiel Elycs (known on social media as @elycs_emiell).

The collection is a direct collaboration featuring his raw, unfiltered photographs of the Belgian rave scene, which are incorporated into the garments.

One standout example is the colourful and eye-catching Wasted Paris Fusion Puffer Jacket.

The visuals and designs are heavily inspired by the energy and aesthetic of early 2000s British rave culture.

For this project Wasted Paris approched me and asked me to use one of my photo’s for their capsule.

Later i also made a promotion video to launch the puffer jacket

For this video i casted the person that was in the original photo.

This really was one of the projects that brought me where i am today, It let me see what i didn’t knew was possible.

Cadavre Exquis

Documenting my life and the people around me

Analog Photography

A life that feels like a cadavre exquis is best described as a sequence of disparate fragments, unexpected turns, and events that seem perfectly logical in the moment, yet form a bizarre and incoherent whole when viewed in retrospect.

Because this surrealist artistic technique relies on blindly adding to someone else’s preceding line or drawing, such a life often lacks a clear, unifying thread or an overarching script.

This is my passion project for myself.
I wanna make a trilogy book series out of this.
Why a trilogy u ask, whell a great story isn’t told in one film or one book. It’s told in 3.

Each book will contain 5 years of analog photography documented by me myself.
